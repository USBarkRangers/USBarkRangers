US BARK Rangers

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how US BARK Rangers (the "App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and
 protects your information when you use the Bark Ranger iOS application and related services (the "Service"). By using
 the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign in, we collect your email address and basic
 profile details provided through your chosen sign-in method (such as Sign in with Apple or email
 authentication).

Your activity in the App: Parks and sites you mark as visited, saved places, trip stops and notes,
walk and expedition records, scores, badges, settings, and similar progress data you create.

User content: Trip notes and saved-place information are stored with your account. Images you select
for the watermark tool are processed on your device, not uploaded to our servers by that tool.

Location: If you grant permission, we use your device location to center the map and
 show nearby parks and support walks you choose to record. If you mark a location-verified visit,
the app sends the location fix to the backend to validate proximity. Your saved places and trip stops can
contain location information. You can decline or revoke location access in iOS Settings.

Payment information: If you make a purchase, payment details are collected and
 processed by Apple. Our native backend verifies Apple's signed transactions and stores subscription
identifiers, status, expiration and the link to your Bark account, including redeemed offers.
We do not receive or store full card numbers.

Technical data: Service-request and security information needed to operate the app, verify requests
and prevent abuse. The native app uses Firebase App Check for app integrity. Settings, downloaded
park information, account-scoped data and pending changes are stored on your device for offline use.
The native app does not include the web app's Google Analytics or GoatCounter integration.

2. How We Use Information

To provide, operate, and improve the Service and your saved progress.

To authenticate you and keep your account secure.

To process purchases and provide premium features.

To respond to your requests and provide support.

To protect against fraud, abuse, and unlawful activity, and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Service Providers

We use Google Firebase and Google Cloud for authentication, account storage, backend functions,
public park data and app integrity. Apple supplies Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions,
and MapKit map imagery, place search and routing. Requests to these services are governed by their
applicable privacy terms. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Storage on Your Device

The app stores your preferences and downloaded data on your iPhone, and uses secure system storage
for sign-in credentials. Account caches and pending changes are separated by Bark account. Changes
saved while offline are sent when you reconnect. Deleting the app can remove changes that have not
yet synced; check Pending changes before doing so.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the
 Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data as described below. Some
 information may be retained where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can access and update much of your profile and progress data within the App.

You can control location and photo permissions in iOS Settings.

You can request account deletion in the app's Account screen, or contact us for access or deletion
requests. Deleting a Bark account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage it separately in
your Apple Account settings. Apple retains its payment records under its own policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information
 from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can
 remove it.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is
 completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above, and
 material changes may be highlighted in the App. Your continued use of the Service after changes take
 effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
